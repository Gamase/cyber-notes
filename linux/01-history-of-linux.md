# History of Linux

> Source: Linux Journey – Grasshopper L1 · 2026-11-16

## Key Points
- UNIX (1969, Thompson & Ritchie, Bell Labs) → they rewrote UNIX in C (1973), which made it portable.
- GNU (Stallman, 1983) built most of a free OS, but was missing a kernel.
- Linus Torvalds wrote the kernel (1991) and moved it to the GPL in 1992.
- "Linux" is technically just the kernel; the full system is GNU/Linux.

## Reflections
- It's pretty awesome how many things had to happen before Linux became one of the most important operating systems in the world. A lot of people give Torvalds all the credit, but he couldn't have done it without the contributions of Ken Thompson and Stallman. I didn't know that GNU was almost a full operating system on its own. Today's distributions combine the Linux kernel with GNU tools and other software, and they complement each other. That's why it's called GNU/Linux: it's the collaborative work of GNU and the kernel.
